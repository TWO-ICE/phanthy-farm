## #1 · 推荐（默认）

**用途**：突出"透明机甲 + RGB 氛围"DNA，呼应"9.9 元捡漏"科技感。

```
A product hero shot of a single transparent mecha-style small bluetooth speaker with visible internal circuitry and RGB lighting glowing softly in cyan and magenta. The speaker is placed diagonally on a dark matte surface, with subtle RGB light reflecting on the surface beneath. Beside it: a small kraft paper tag with the number "9.9" printed in bold black marker. Dark gradient background, dramatic studio lighting with RGB accent. 1:1 square aspect ratio, photorealistic, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, cyberpunk aesthetic. No text overlay, no watermark, no readable brand logo.
```

**关键控制点**：1:1 / 透明外壳 + RGB 光晕 = 核心视觉 / "9.9" 写在小纸片上 / 不要可识别的荣耀/HONOR 字样。
